## Early viruses
- Brain (1986): first PC virus
- Morris worm (1988): by Robert Morris, meant to gauge the size of the internet.
  A bug made it reinstall itself again and again, crashing computers.

## Modern attacks
- Love Letter (2000): email worm
- Equifax (2017): a data breach, not a virus

## Virus vs worm
- Virus: needs a host (file or program) and a person to run it
- Worm: spreads by itself across the network

## Phishing types
- Phishing: mass fake messages
- Spear phishing: aimed at a specific person or small group
- Whaling: aimed at executives
- Watering hole: attacker compromises a real site their targets visit
- Impersonation: a fake site copying a real one

## Threat actors
- Hackers: ethical, semi-ethical, unethical
- Hacktivists, insiders, advanced persistent threats (APTs)

## Attack types
- Password, social engineering, physical, adversarial AI, supply chain
- Cryptographic: birthday, collision, downgrade
